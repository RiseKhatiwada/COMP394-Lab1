## 2. Game Elements of One of My Favorite Games

### Game Selected: Grand Theft Auto V

### Objects

The objects in Grand Theft Auto V include the player character, vehicles, weapons, money, buildings, pedestrians, police vehicles, and other items found throughout the game world. These objects are used by the player to interact with and progress through the game.

### Actions

The actions in Grand Theft Auto V include walking, running, jumping, driving vehicles, shooting, fighting, interacting with characters, purchasing items, completing missions, and exploring the game world. Players can also switch between the three main characters during the story.

### Play Space

The play space is the open-world map of Grand Theft Auto V. It includes the city of Los Santos, the surrounding countryside, mountains, highways, beaches, buildings, and other areas that players can explore.

### Players

The player controls a character in the game and interacts with the game world. In the story mode, the player can control Michael, Franklin, and Trevor. Grand Theft Auto V also includes an online mode where multiple players can interact in the same game world.

### Goals

The goals in Grand Theft Auto V include completing missions, progressing through the story, earning money, and completing objectives given by different characters. Players can also explore the open world and participate in optional activities. In the story, the characters work toward completing different missions and major heists.

### Rules

The rules define how the game can be played and what actions are possible. Players must interact with the game world using the available game mechanics. Vehicles can be driven, weapons can be used, and characters can move around the game world. The player can lose health when taking damage, and certain actions can cause the police to pursue the player. Missions also have specific objectives that must be completed to progress.

## 3. Which Elements Were Easy to Identify and Which Were Hard?

### Elements That Were Easy to Identify

Objects, actions, and play space were the easiest elements to identify. The objects are easy to recognize because they are visible during gameplay, such as cars, weapons, characters, buildings, and money. The actions are also easy to identify because they are the things the player directly does, such as walking, driving, shooting, and completing missions. The play space was easy to identify because Grand Theft Auto V has a large open-world environment where most of the gameplay takes place.

### Elements That Were Hard to Identify

Goals and rules were somewhat harder to identify. The main goals can be identified through the story missions, but the game also contains many optional activities, so there is not always one single goal for the player. The rules were harder because many of them are built into the game's mechanics rather than being directly explained to the player. For example, the player learns how police pursuits, health, vehicles, weapons, and mission objectives work through gameplay.

Overall, the six elements can be found in Grand Theft Auto V, but some are easier to recognize than others because they are more directly visible during gameplay.
